## Important source distinctions

- Class 3 slide 35 says approximately **60–70% of allocated release time** for MRFs as a rule of thumb. This is not a percentage of feature count or a Scrum requirement. The wording now explicitly identifies the context.
- Class 2’s transcript acknowledges conflicting Retrospective figures. The notes use **three hours for a month-long Sprint**, matching slide 39 and the 2020 Guide. Shorter-Sprint event durations are not presented as a universally mandatory proportional formula.
- Five-to-nine development-team sizing in Class 5 slide 30 is an older guideline; the typical ten-or-fewer Scrum Team wording in slide 19 refers to the entire Scrum Team under the 2020 Guide.
- DEEP, MoSCoW, story points, Planning Poker, and spikes are not presented as additional mandatory Scrum events or artifacts.
- The fixed time/cost/quality framing is identified with the DSDM/classroom context, rather than being imposed as a universal Scrum budgeting rule.
